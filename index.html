Discover Himachal Pradesh

Holiday Destinations

Shimla

Shimla is the capital of Himachal Pradesh, known for its stunning mountain views, colonial architecture, and vibrant bazaars.

Manali

Manali is a picturesque hill station, famous for its snow-capped mountains and adventure sports such as skiing and paragliding.

Dharamshala

Dharamshala is known for its Tibetan culture and home to the Dalai Lama. It's a serene place with beautiful landscapes.

Kasol

Kasol is located in the Parvati Valley. It's renowned for its laid-back atmosphere and stunning natural beauty.

Spiti Valley

Spiti Valley is known for its breathtaking landscapes and ancient monasteries, an adventure lover's paradise.
